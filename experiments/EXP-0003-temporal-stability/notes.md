# EXP-0003 — Temporal Stability Review

Status: Reviewed retrospective temporal diagnostic

## Research integrity

EXP-0003 does **not** claim out-of-sample validation.

EXP-0001 and EXP-0002 already exposed aggregate results covering 2021-01-01 through 2026-08-31. Splitting that same history into calendar folds after those experiments cannot make the data unseen again.

The purpose of EXP-0003 is narrower: determine whether the frozen v1 result is spread across multiple calendar regimes or concentrated in a subset of them.

A separate prospective holdout was preregistered before its future start date under `experiments/PROSPECTIVE-v1-holdout/protocol.yaml`.

## Predeclared questions

EXP-0003 preregistered two simple stability thresholds before the real-data run:

1. expectancy positive in at least four of six folds;
2. profit factor above 1 in at least four of six folds.

Both thresholds failed. Only three folds met each condition.

The known weak periods were also confirmed independently:

- 2025 had negative return and negative expectancy;
- 2026-YTD had negative return and negative expectancy.

The project must not redefine the thresholds after seeing the result.

## The 2022 no-trade fold

2022 produced zero trades, zero exposure, zero return, and zero drawdown.

This is not a losing year. The market-regime and setup rules kept the strategy out of the market for the full fold. That behavior may be useful defensively, but it does not satisfy the preregistered requirement for positive expectancy or profit factor because no trades occurred.

The distinction is retained rather than post-hoc changing the pass criteria.

## Regime dependence

The full-period EXP-0001 result hides substantial temporal concentration.

The positive historical expectancy is generated by 2021, 2023, and 2024. The strategy was inactive in 2022 and weak in 2025 and 2026-YTD.

Profit concentration inside winning folds is also high:

- 2021 top-five-profit share: about 85%;
- 2023: about 95%;
- 2024: about 86%;
- 2025: 100% of positive PnL came from the small set of winners that existed.

This is consistent with a trend-following system whose aggregate edge is driven by a small number of large trends, but it means the headline +1.05R expectancy should not be interpreted as uniformly available through time.

## Relationship to EXP-0001

The six fold runs contain 65 closed trades in total, matching the EXP-0001 trade count. Their trade-count-weighted expectancy is also effectively the same as the EXP-0001 aggregate.

That is expected: EXP-0003 decomposes previously observed history. It provides information about **where** the historical result came from, not new independent evidence that the edge will persist.

## Provider revision note

BTC-USD and SOL-USD source hashes remain stable across recent experiments. The adjusted META, NVDA, and QQQ hashes changed again relative to the EXP-0002 snapshot.

This reinforces the existing project rule that provider data must be fingerprinted and that a hash detects revisions but cannot reconstruct a prior provider snapshot.

No strategy parameter was changed because of these revisions or because of the fold results.

## Prospective holdout

The first genuine v1 prospective holdout is preregistered to begin on `2026-09-14`, after this protocol was written.

Validation claims are blocked until both conditions are met:

- at least 24 calendar months have elapsed, with minimum observation end `2028-09-14`;
- at least 30 trades have closed.

Interim monitoring is allowed, but interim performance must not be used to tune v1. Any strategy or risk change requires a new prospective protocol/version.

## Decision

`PREDECLARED TEMPORAL-STABILITY THRESHOLDS NOT MET — CONTINUE RESEARCH — DO NOT DEPLOY`

EXP-0003 reduces confidence that the historical edge is temporally uniform. It does not erase the positive aggregate result or the execution-cost robustness found in EXP-0002, but it makes regime dependence explicit.

The v1 strategy remains frozen for the prospective holdout.

## Next research step

While the prospective holdout accumulates genuinely new data, the next safe research work should add passive/reference benchmarks and then test parameter-neighborhood robustness and a broader universe without retroactively modifying v1.
